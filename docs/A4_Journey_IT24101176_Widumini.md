### 2. Key Technical Hurdle Overcome
* **The Problem:** Raw machine learning outputs (e.g., continuous probability scores or abstract ROC-AUC numbers) cannot be directly executed by non-technical frontline customer success teams. Blanket promotional discounting to all flagged churners would dilute corporate profit margins and train customers to threaten cancellation.
* **The Solution:** Architected an operational 3-Tier Risk Segmentation matrix dividing customers into Critical Risk ($P \ge 0.65$), Moderate Risk ($0.40 \le P < 0.65$), and Safe accounts ($P < 0.40$). Designed tailored, low-cost interventions (such as \$10 auto-pay switch credits and free 6-month value add-on trials) that address the root causes of churn without margin dilution.
